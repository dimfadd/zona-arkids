# Prompt logo: Roket Kertas (dulu "Calistung AR for Kids")

Pakai di generator logo AI. Buat **simbol saja**; tulisan nama dipasang terpisah oleh desainer
dengan Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, karena generator AI sering salah mengeja teks.

## Prompt utama (bahasa Inggris, hasilnya lebih stabil)

```
Logo symbol for "Calistung AR for Kids", an Indonesian learning product for children aged 4 to 7
who are learning to read, write and count. Children colour a printed line drawing on paper with
crayons; then a parent photographs the page and the child's own drawing pops up from the paper as
a standing paper cut-out with a white sticker-like border.

Concept: a page from an Indonesian school exercise book (white paper, light-blue horizontal ruling,
one red vertical margin line). Part of the page is cut out along a simple shape and folded up so it
stands on the page, leaving a cut-out hole behind it and a short soft shadow.

Style: flat 2D vector, thick confident brown-black outline like a children's colouring-book drawing,
simple geometric shapes, generous white space. Palette limited to: paper white #FFFFFF,
ink #26231F, ruling blue #2F5FA8, margin red #C4302B. At most one area with a light crayon texture.
Must stay readable as a 48x48 px app icon. Centered on a plain white background.

Avoid: gradients, 3D render, glossy or glass effects, glow, sparkles, stars, rainbow colours,
cartoon mascot with huge eyes, phones, AR goggles, holograms, any text or letters other than the
one shape described.
```

## Tiga arah bentuk (ganti kalimat "simple shape" di atas)

- **A. Huruf yang berdiri:** `the cut-out shape is a lowercase single-storey letter "a" (schoolbook handwriting form), folded up from the ruled page`
- **B. Sapi kertas:** `the cut-out shape is a very simple cow silhouette (four legs, two horns), folded up from the ruled page`
- **C. Angka dan huruf:** `two small cut-outs stand side by side on the page: the number "1" and a single-storey letter "a"`
